There were some common problems that we saw in the market with party bags:

- Crystals or other embellishments are loosely attached, so they come off easily.

- Bags usually tug with your dresses easily, which either ruins your dress, bag, or MOOD when using them.

- Lack of functionality — it’s the worst when you can’t even fit your phone in your bag! What’s the point? You are never stepping out without your phone, for sure!

Keeping this in mind, we designed our gorgeous party clutch bag, KELLY.

The stunning bag is an incredible crystal bag that is sure to catch attention because of its luxurious finish. The crystals are embellished so that neither the stones come off nor do they tug on your dress.

The bag can be worn in three ways: as a clutch, mini bag (with a short chain strap attached), or crossbody bag (with a long chain strap provided that is adjustable with a slider). It is a versatile bag that is functional for different occasions. You can wear the clutch for your prom and the same as mini bag on a date night.

Moreover, even if the bag may seem small, its wide base allows it to be very spacious. It can easily hold your phone, your touch-up makeup, a mini-wallet, etc.

It is a designer bag well-suited for all ages; it’s a keeper for yourself and also a beautiful accessory to gift to someone special!
